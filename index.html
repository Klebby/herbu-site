The Artist

Why “Herbu”?

I'm Kaleb Allen, a sculptor in Austin, Texas. I sign my work Herbu because my raison d'être calls me “Boo”. I'm her Boo, so I'm Herbu.

I work in whatever material a piece calls for: marble and stone carved by hand, hardwoods for functional pieces, and 3D scanning and printing for portraits and gifts. Every piece is one of a kind.

◆ Based in Austin, Texas · local pickup available
